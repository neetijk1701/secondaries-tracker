Privacy policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Secondaries Tracker (“the app”) is a free tool for medical school applicants, available at secondaries-tracker.wusm-tools.workers.dev. In short: your schools and essays stay in your browser and, if you turn it on, in your own Google Drive. The app has no server or database of its own, does not collect your essays, and does not use analytics or advertising.

What the app stores, and where

  • In your browser. The schools, prompts, essays, base essays, dates, and settings you enter are saved in your browser’s local storage on your device. They are not sent to the app’s developer.
  • In your Google Drive, only if you connect it. If you click “Save to Google Drive,” the app saves a copy of that same data as one file in a hidden app-specific folder in your own Google Drive. Only this app can see that folder, and the app cannot see any of your other Drive files.
  • Backup files. If you click “Download backup,” the app saves a file to your device. What you do with it is up to you.

Google user data

If you connect Google Drive, the app asks Google for these permissions:

  • Google Drive app data (drive.appdata): to create, read, update, and delete the app’s own data file in its hidden app folder in your Drive. It cannot access any other files.
  • Your email address and basic sign-in (email, openid): to show you which Google account your work is saving to.

This data goes directly between your browser and Google. It is not sent to, stored by, or shared with the app’s developer or anyone else. It is used only to save and load your own work. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train AI or machine-learning models. The app’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services the app relies on

  • Cloudflare hosts the app’s files. Like any web host, it handles basic request information such as your IP address to deliver the pages. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts supplies the app’s typefaces, and Google Identity Services provides the sign-in window. Your browser contacts Google to load them. See Google’s privacy policy.

The app does not use cookies of its own, analytics, trackers, or ads.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Google Drive from the app’s footer at any time. Your work stays in your browser.
  • Remove the app’s access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete your data. “Erase everything” in the app’s footer deletes your data from your browser and, if connected, from the app’s Drive folder. You can also delete the app’s hidden Drive data in Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps → Secondaries Tracker → Delete hidden app data. Clearing your browser’s site data removes the browser copy.

Children

The app is meant for medical school applicants and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
